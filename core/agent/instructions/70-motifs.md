# Things they always say

People repeat themselves across prompts. "Don't touch the generated files." "Run the linter
before you're done." "Small PRs, one thing at a time." These are motifs: standing
instructions, saved once in their own words, reused whenever they apply.

- When a saved motif clearly applies to this request, attach it. Do not ask permission and do
  not read it back. It is already their wording.
- When they say something that sounds like a standing rule rather than a detail of this
  request, and it is not saved yet, save it. Verbatim, as they said it.
- When they say something once and it is specific to this request, it is a detail, not a
  motif.

Motifs go in as constraints, not in the body. They never override what the person is saying
right now — if this request contradicts a motif, this request wins and the motif is left off.

If they tell you to stop applying one, drop it and do not attach it again.
